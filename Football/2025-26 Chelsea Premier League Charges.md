# Background

- In May 2022, a consortium led by Todd Boehly and Clearlake Capital completed the £4.25bn acquisition of Chelsea Football Club from Roman Abramovich. Shortly after the takeover, the new ownership voluntarily self-reported potential historical financial irregularities to the Premier League, The Football Association, and UEFA.
    
      
    - `The club voluntarily and proactively disclosed to all applicable regulators potential historical rule breaches, including incomplete financial reporting that took place over a decade ago.` — [Club statement on Premier League settlement, Chelsea FC, 2026](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/club-statement-on-premier-league-settlement?utm_source=gemini)
        
          
        
    - `In 2022, Chelsea FC's current owners voluntarily reported to the League that they had evidence of potential breaches of Premier League Rules.` — [Premier League Statement, Premier League, 2026](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4616198/premier-league-statement-on-chelsea-sanctions-march-2026?utm_source=gemini)
        
          
        
- On 28 July 2023, UEFA announced it had reached a €10m (£8.6m) settlement with Chelsea after the club's self-reporting revealed incomplete financial information submitted under Abramovich's ownership between 2012 and 2019.
    
      
    - `The CFCB First Chamber concluded a settlement agreement with Chelsea FC in respect of incomplete financial reporting submitted under the club's previous ownership.` — [UEFA CFCB settlement update, UEFA, 2023](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/0283-189f3876be8b-3e5f49001000/&utm_source=gemini)
        
          
        
- In late 2023 and into 2024, investigative reports revealed tens of millions of pounds in offshore payments made by companies owned by Roman Abramovich to agents, players, and third parties associated with transfers between 2011 and 2018.
    
      
    - `More than two years ago, we revealed tens of millions of pounds in secretive payments had been made from offshore firms owned by Roman Abramovich during his time as owner of Chelsea. Some of these related to the acquisition of major stars including Eden Hazard, Samuel Eto'o and Willian.` — [Chelsea given record fine for payments revealed by Bureau investigation, TBIJ, 2026](https://www.thebureauinvestigates.com/stories/2026-03-16/chelsea-given-record-fine-for-payments-revealed-in-bureau-investigation?utm_source=gemini)
        
          
        
- In 2025, Chelsea made a further voluntary disclosure to the Premier League regarding potential breaches of Youth Development Rules committed by a former senior employee between 2019 and 2022.
    
      
    - `This followed a further voluntary report by the club in 2025... made immediately after the club had received documents from a third-party (unrelated to the club) which evidenced potential breaches of the Rules.` — [Premier League Statement, Premier League, 2026](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4616198/premier-league-statement-on-chelsea-sanctions-march-2026?utm_source=gemini)
        
          
        

# The Charges

- On 11 September 2025, The Football Association formally charged Chelsea FC with 74 separate breaches of FA Regulations relating to historical transactions spanning from 2009 to 2022.
    
      
    - `The Football Association has today charged Chelsea FC with breaches of Regulations J1 and C2 of The FA Football Agents Regulations, Regulations A2 and A3 of The FA Regulations on Working with Intermediaries, and Regulations A1 and B3 of The FA Third Party Investment in Players Regulations. In total, 74 charges have been brought against Chelsea FC.` — [Chelsea FC charged with breaching FA Regulations, The FA, 2025](https://www.thefa.com/news/2025/sep/11/chelsea-fc-update-110925?utm_source=gemini)
        
          
        
    - `The conduct that is the subject of the charges ranges from 2009 to 2022 and primarily relates to events which occurred between the 2010/11 to 2015/16 playing seasons.` — [Chelsea FC charged with breaching FA Regulations, The FA, 2025](https://www.thefa.com/news/2025/sep/11/chelsea-fc-update-110925?utm_source=gemini)
        
          
        
- During the investigation, the Premier League conducted an extensive financial analysis to establish whether the undisclosed offshore payments would have caused Chelsea to breach Profitability and Sustainability Rules (PSR).
    
      
    - `The Premier League assessed a series of recalculations of the club's historical financial submissions which took into account the payments made for the benefit of Chelsea FC. Importantly, having undertaken that assessment, the Premier League Board was satisfied that in no scenario would the club have breached the League's Profitability and Sustainability Rules during the relevant periods.` — [Premier League Statement, Premier League, 2026](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4616198/premier-league-statement-on-chelsea-sanctions-march-2026?utm_source=gemini)
        
          
        
    - `The club wishes to make clear that following robust financial analysis by the Premier League, it was concluded that “in no scenario would the club have exceeded the maximum allowable loss of £105 million over the three-year assessment period in the Rules.”` — [Club statement on Premier League settlement, Chelsea FC, 2026](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/club-statement-on-premier-league-settlement?utm_source=gemini)
        
          
        

# Settlement

- On 16 March 2026, the Premier League and Chelsea FC agreed to two separate sanction agreements, ratified by an independent Commission, resolving all historical financial and youth registration investigations.
    
      
    - `The Premier League has concluded two separate disciplinary processes involving Chelsea Football Club, following the Club voluntarily self-reporting potential historical breaches of rules.`
        
          
        
        `Two sanction agreements have been ratified by an independent Commission under which Chelsea FC has accepted fines totalling £10.75million for breaches of the Premier League's rules relating to Financial Reporting, Third Party Investment and Youth Development.` — [Premier League Statement, Premier League, 2026](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4616198/premier-league-statement-on-chelsea-sanctions-march-2026?utm_source=gemini)
        
          
        
- The terms of the Premier League settlement imposed financial penalties alongside sporting restrictions on Chelsea's first-team and academy operations.
    
      
    - `The Premier League and Chelsea FC have now entered into a sanction agreement under which the club accepts a £10million fine and a suspended one-year first team transfer ban (suspended for two years).` — [Premier League Statement, Premier League, 2026](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4616198/premier-league-statement-on-chelsea-sanctions-march-2026?utm_source=gemini)
        
          
        
    - `As a result of this additional investigation, a separate sanction agreement has been entered into with Chelsea FC, under which the club has accepted an immediate nine-month ban from registering Academy players from Premier League and EFL clubs. The club will also pay a £750,000 fine.` — [Premier League Statement, Premier League, 2026](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4616198/premier-league-statement-on-chelsea-sanctions-march-2026?utm_source=gemini)
        
          
        
    - `The Premier League Board noted that the club's proactive self-reporting, admissions of breach and exceptional cooperation throughout the investigation acted as significant mitigating factors.` — [Premier League Statement, Premier League, 2026](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4616198/premier-league-statement-on-chelsea-sanctions-march-2026?utm_source=gemini)
        
          
        

# Aftermath

- On 31 July 2026, The Football Association concluded its separate disciplinary proceedings after Chelsea admitted to all 74 charges, resulting in a fine and a suspended registration ban upon appeal.
    
      
    - `A financial sanction of £10 million and a suspended two-window registration ban have been imposed on Chelsea FC for breaches of The Football Association [The FA] Football Agent Regulations, Regulations on Working with Intermediaries and Third Party Investment in Players Regulations.` — [Chelsea FC update - The FA, The FA, 2026](https://www.thefa.com/news/2026/jul/31/chelsea-sanctioned-31072026?utm_source=gemini)
        
          
        
    - `Chelsea FC admitted to the 74 breaches of FA Rule E1.2 prior to a hearing and an independent Regulatory Commission imposed a six-point deduction, which was to be suspended until 30 June 2027, and a £10 million fine. The club appealed against the suspended points deduction and an independent Appeal Board allowed the appeal and set aside this sanction... In its place, the Appeal Board imposed a registration ban for two complete and consecutive transfer windows which is suspended until 30 June 2027.` — [Chelsea FC update - The FA, The FA, 2026](https://www.thefa.com/news/2026/jul/31/chelsea-sanctioned-31072026?utm_source=gemini)
