# Financial Background

- 29 May 2022: Nottingham Forest returned to the Premier League after a 23-year absence, creating the financial pressure of rebuilding a Championship squad for top-flight football.
    
    - `Nottingham Forest secured promotion to the Premier League by beating Huddersfield Town 1-0 in the Championship play-off final at Wembley.`
        
    
    `Forest had spent 23 years outside the top flight before Steve Cooper's side completed the promotion campaign.` — [Nottingham Forest: How Reds went from 'fearful' to 'phenomenal' to return to Premier League, BBC Sport, 2022](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/61579779)
    
- Summer 2022: Forest undertook an unprecedented recruitment programme after promotion, signing 21 players and making five loan additions.
    
    - `Forest signed 22 new players that summer and made five loan signings as they attempted to rebuild their squad for the Premier League.`
        
    
    `Their net transfer spend over the 2022-23 season was £142.8m.`
    
    `The scale of the spending was significant because Forest had only just returned to the Premier League and therefore had to construct a squad capable of competing at a substantially higher level.` — [Nottingham Forest points deduction: Loss of four points drops club into relegation zone, BBC Sport, 2024](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/68594865)
    
- 2022/23: Forest's spending contributed to a difficult PSR position, while the club's previous Championship seasons meant its permitted losses were lower than the normal Premier League threshold.
    
    - `A club would ordinarily be permitted to lose £105m over the relevant three-year period.`
        
    
    `For Forest, however, the threshold was reduced because two seasons of the relevant period had been spent in the Championship. The applicable threshold was therefore £61m.`
    
    `The reduction was £22m for each season spent in the Championship.` — [Everton and Nottingham Forest confirm Premier League PSR breaches, Premier League, 2024](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/3858986)
    
- 31 March 2023: Forest submitted a projected PSR calculation to the Premier League as it attempted to establish its position before the financial year ended.
    
    - `The club submitted its projected calculation before the end of the financial year, with the final position dependent on its accounts and permitted adjustments.`
        
    
    `The subsequent proceedings examined the calculation covering the relevant three-year period and the circumstances that led to Forest exceeding its permitted loss.` — [Further statement and analysis of Profit and Sustainability Rules, Nottingham Forest Supporters' Trust, 2024](https://nffctrust.org/post/further-statement-and-analysis-of-profit-and-sustainability-rules)
    
- Spring 2023: Forest identified Brennan Johnson as the principal player whose sale could generate enough profit to bring the club within the PSR limit.
    
    - `Because Johnson had come through Forest's academy, a large proportion of any transfer fee would be treated as profit for PSR purposes.`
        
    
    `The Commission's evidence recorded that Forest knew it needed to generate approximately £30m of player-sale profit before the end of the financial year.`
    
    `Johnson was therefore central to Forest's attempts to bring its PSR position into compliance before 30 June.` — [Seeing the Wood for the Trees, Football Law, 2024](https://www.footballlaw.co.uk/articles/forest-psr-breach-commission-seeing-wood-for-trees)
    

# The June 2023 Deadline

- 30 June 2023: Forest reached the PSR accounting deadline without completing a sale of Johnson.
    
    - `The 30 June accounting date was crucial because Forest's PSR calculation for the relevant financial year was determined by its position at the end of that period.`
        
    
    `Forest's eventual sale of Johnson could not be included in the calculation because the transfer did not take place until the following financial year.` — [Further statement and analysis of Profit and Sustainability Rules, Nottingham Forest Supporters' Trust, 2024](https://nffctrust.org/post/further-statement-and-analysis-of-profit-and-sustainability-rules)
    
- 30 June 2023: Atlético Madrid submitted a conditional €50m offer for Johnson on the final day of the financial year.
    
    - `On 30 June 2023, the last day of FY23, Forest received an email from Atlético with the subject line "Offer".`
        
    
    `The proposed transfer fee was €50m (£42.9m), but the proposal was expressly subject to a number of conditions, including reaching an agreement with the player, the player passing a medical and Spanish league registration requirements.`
    
    `Forest responded by making a counter-proposal of €65m (£55.8m), together with increases to some of the proposed contingent bonuses.` — [Independent Commission written reasons, Nottingham Forest PSR case, 2024](https://svenhaist.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Die-Urteilsbegruendung-zum-Punktabzug-fuer-Nottingham-Forest.pdf)
    
- 30 June 2023: The Atlético negotiations did not result in a completed transfer, leaving Forest above the PSR threshold at the end of the accounting period.
    
    - `The Commission found that the discussions following Forest's counter-proposal did not progress further.`
        
    
    `The significance of the Atlético approach became central to Forest's later mitigation argument: the club argued that it had come close to completing a transaction capable of solving its PSR problem, while the Premier League argued that no transfer had actually been completed by the relevant deadline.` — [Independent Commission written reasons, Nottingham Forest PSR case, 2024](https://svenhaist.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Die-Urteilsbegruendung-zum-Punktabzug-fuer-Nottingham-Forest.pdf)
    
- July–August 2023: Forest received further offers for Johnson but rejected them before eventually selling him to Tottenham.
    
    - `Brentford submitted offers of £32.5m, £35m and £40m for Johnson during July and August.`
        
    
    `Forest rejected each of those offers before the player eventually moved to Tottenham Hotspur.` — [Further statement and analysis of Profit and Sustainability Rules, Nottingham Forest Supporters' Trust, 2024](https://nffctrust.org/post/further-statement-and-analysis-of-profit-and-sustainability-rules)
    
- 1 September 2023: Johnson was sold to Tottenham for £47.5m on the final day of the transfer window.
    
    - `Tottenham confirmed the £47.5m signing of Brennan Johnson from Nottingham Forest on the stroke of the 11pm transfer deadline.`
        
    
    `The transfer followed a week of negotiations between the clubs and resulted in Johnson signing a six-year contract with Tottenham.` — [Tottenham agree £47m deal with Nottingham Forest for Brennan Johnson, The Guardian, 2023](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2023/sep/01/tottenham-agree-47m-deal-with-nottingham-forest-for-brennan-johnson)
    
    `The sale was ultimately worth £47.5m, but because it occurred after 30 June it could not be used to reduce the PSR calculation for the financial year that had already ended.` — [Further statement and analysis of Profit and Sustainability Rules, Nottingham Forest Supporters' Trust, 2024](https://nffctrust.org/post/further-statement-and-analysis-of-profit-and-sustainability-rules)
    
- 31 December 2023: Forest submitted its final PSR calculation and acknowledged that it had exceeded the permitted loss.
    
    - `Forest's initial submitted calculation showed a PSR loss of approximately £86.8m.`
        
    
    `The club admitted a breach of the PSR and sought early discussions with the Premier League concerning the extent of the breach and the possible sanction.` — [A four-point deduction — what next for Nottingham Forest?, The Famous Club, 2024](https://thefamousclub.co.uk/2024/03/18/four-point-deduction-what-next-nottingham-forest/)
    

# The Premier League Case

- 15 January 2024: Forest formally admitted breaching the Premier League's Profitability and Sustainability Rules and was referred to an independent Commission.
    
    - `Everton FC and Nottingham Forest FC have each confirmed to the Premier League that they are in breach of the League's Profitability and Sustainability Rules (PSR).`
        
    
    `This is as a result of sustaining losses above the permitted thresholds for the assessment period ending Season 2022/23.`
    
    `In accordance with Premier League Rules, both cases have now been referred to the chair of the Judicial Panel, who will appoint separate Commissions to determine the appropriate sanction.` — [Everton and Nottingham Forest confirm Premier League PSR breaches, Premier League, 2024](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/3858986)
    
- 15 January 2024: The applicable threshold for Forest was confirmed as £61m because two seasons of the assessment period had been spent in the Championship.
    
    - `A club will be in breach of the PSRs if its PSR Calculation over the relevant period results in a loss in excess of £105 million.`
        
    
    `That threshold was reduced by £22 million for each season that a club had been in the Championship during the relevant period.`
    
    `Forest therefore had an applicable threshold of £61m.` — [Nottingham Forest FC deducted four points by independent Commission, Premier League, 2024](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/3936397)
    
- January–March 2024: The Premier League's final calculation put Forest's PSR loss at £95.536m, £34.536m above the permitted £61m.
    
    - `The Premier League's case was that Forest's PSR Calculation, dated 31 December 2023, demonstrated losses in excess of the £61m threshold applicable to Forest.`
        
    
    `The Premier League considered that Forest's PSR Calculation resulted in a loss of £95,536,000, breaching the £61m threshold by £34,536,000.`
    
    `Forest accepts that it is in breach of the PSR but asserts that this ought to be considered in light of certain exceptional circumstances.` — [Independent Commission written reasons, Nottingham Forest PSR case, 2024](https://svenhaist.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Die-Urteilsbegruendung-zum-Punktabzug-fuer-Nottingham-Forest.pdf)
    
- January–March 2024: Forest's defence concentrated on six mitigating factors, with the Johnson transfer described by the club as its principal or "golden" mitigation.
    
    - `Forest based its case around six points of mitigation, all of which were disputed by the Premier League.`
        
    
    `The sale of Brennan Johnson to Tottenham was described as the "golden mitigation" because the transfer generated a substantial fee shortly after the PSR deadline.`
    
    `Forest argued that selling Johnson later had allowed the club to obtain a higher fee than it would have received had it completed a transaction before 30 June.` — [Nottingham Forest points deduction: Loss of four points drops club into relegation zone, BBC Sport, 2024](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/68594865)
    
- January–March 2024: Forest also argued that its circumstances as a newly promoted club should be taken into account.
    
    - `Forest argued that it was in a unique position because it had spent the preceding two seasons in the Championship.`
        
    
    `The club also pointed to the fact that it had not received parachute payments following promotion, while the PSR rules nevertheless reduced its permitted loss because of its Championship seasons.` — [Nottingham Forest docked four points for Premier League financial rules breach, The Guardian, 2024](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2024/mar/18/nottingham-forest-docked-four-points-premier-league-financial-rules-breach-profitability-and-sustainability)
    
- 7–8 March 2024: The independent Commission heard the case over two days.
    
    - `The parties' cases were pleaded in the Complaint dated 15 January 2024, the Answer dated 30 January 2024 and the Amended Answer dated 26 February 2024.`
        
    
    `The Hearing took place on 7 and 8 March 2024.` — [Independent Commission written reasons, Nottingham Forest PSR case, 2024](https://svenhaist.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Die-Urteilsbegruendung-zum-Punktabzug-fuer-Nottingham-Forest.pdf)
    

# The Commission Decision

- 18 March 2024: The independent Commission imposed an immediate four-point deduction on Forest.
    
    - `An independent Commission has applied an immediate four-point deduction to Nottingham Forest FC for a breach of the Premier League's Profitability and Sustainability Rules (PSRs) for the period ending Season 2022/23.`
        
    
    `Nottingham Forest was referred to an independent Commission on 15 January, following an admission by the club that it had breached the relevant PSR threshold of £61 million by £34.5 million.` — [Nottingham Forest FC deducted four points by independent Commission, Premier League, 2024](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/3936397)
    
- 18 March 2024: The Commission's starting point was a six-point deduction, but two points were removed because of Forest's early admission and cooperation.
    
    - `The Commission initially considered a six-point deduction appropriate for the size of the breach.`
        
    
    `That starting point was reduced by two points in recognition of Forest's early admission and its cooperation with the Premier League.` — [Nottingham Forest docked four points for Premier League financial rules breach, The Guardian, 2024](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2024/mar/18/nottingham-forest-docked-four-points-premier-league-financial-rules-breach-profitability-and-sustainability)
    
- 18 March 2024: The Commission specifically recognised Forest's cooperation during the proceedings.
    
    - `The Commission found that the club had demonstrated "exceptional cooperation" in its dealings with the Premier League throughout the process.` — [Nottingham Forest FC deducted four points by independent Commission, Premier League, 2024](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/3936397)
        
- 18 March 2024: The Commission rejected Forest's argument that the eventual Johnson sale represented sufficient mitigation for the breach.
    
    - `Forest described the Johnson transfer as a "near miss" and argued that the sale shortly after the deadline should be taken into account when determining the sanction.`
        
    
    `The Commission rejected that argument, concluding that the timing of the sale was a business decision and did not provide the mitigation Forest sought.` — [Forest Foul Play: Nottingham Forest's run in with the Profitability and Sustainability Rules, Kennedys, 2024](https://www.kennedyslaw.com/en/thought-leadership/article/2024/forest-foul-play-nottingham-forest-s-run-in-with-the-profitability-and-sustainability-rules/)
    
- 18 March 2024: The Commission considered the Atlético approach on 30 June but did not treat the failed transaction as equivalent to completing a sale before the deadline.
    
    - `Forest received the €50m Atlético proposal on 30 June and responded with a €65m counter-proposal.`
        
    
    `The Commission considered the circumstances surrounding the offer and the fact that Forest had other potential buyers available after the deadline.`
    
    `The eventual sale to Tottenham occurred more than two months after the relevant accounting date, and the Commission did not regard the circumstances as sufficient mitigation.` — [Nottingham Forest points deduction: Loss of four points drops club into relegation zone, BBC Sport, 2024](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/68594865)
    
- 18 March 2024: Forest's four-point deduction moved the club into the relegation places at the time it was imposed.
    
    - `The four-point penalty pushed Forest from 17th into the relegation zone, one point behind Luton Town.`
        
    
    `Forest criticised the decision and indicated that it intended to appeal.` — [Nottingham Forest docked four points for Premier League financial rules breach, The Guardian, 2024](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2024/mar/18/nottingham-forest-docked-four-points-premier-league-financial-rules-breach-profitability-and-sustainability)
    

# The Appeal

- 25 March 2024: Forest formally appealed the four-point deduction.
    
    - `Nottingham Forest Football Club has appealed the decision of an independent Commission to impose a four-point deduction on the club, after its admission of a breach of the Premier League's Profitability and Sustainability Rules.`
        
    
    `The club lodged the appeal to the Chair of the Judicial Panel today, who will now appoint an Appeal Board to hear the case.` — [Nottingham Forest FC appeal decision of independent Commission, Premier League, 2024](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/3941350)
    
- 25 March 2024: Forest's appeal challenged the Commission's treatment of the Johnson sale and its decision to impose the deduction immediately.
    
    - `The club argued that the independent Commission had erred in not treating its sale of a high-profile player shortly after the assessment period as a mitigating factor.`
        
    
    `Forest also argued that the Commission had erred in electing not to suspend some or all of the points deduction.` — [Independent Appeal Board decision on Nottingham Forest, Premier League, 2024](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/3999776)
    
- 7 May 2024: The independent Appeal Board rejected Forest's appeal and upheld the four-point deduction.
    
    - `An independent Appeal Board has upheld the decision of a Commission to deduct four points from Nottingham Forest following an admitted breach of the Premier League's Profitability and Sustainability Rules.`
        
    
    `The club's two grounds of appeal were rejected.`
    
    `The Appeal Board found the independent Commission was entitled to immediately impose the sanction it did.` — [Independent Appeal Board decision on Nottingham Forest, Premier League, 2024](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/3999776)
    
- 7 May 2024: The four-point deduction therefore remained in place for the rest of the 2023/24 season.
    
    - `The four-point deduction will therefore remain in place.`
        
    
    `The Premier League subsequently amended the note attached to Forest's league-table position to state that the club had been deducted four points following a breach of the PSRs.` — [Independent Appeal Board decision on Nottingham Forest, Premier League, 2024](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/3999776)
    
- 19 May 2024: Forest finished 17th in the Premier League and retained its top-flight status.
    
    - `Forest's final position was 17th, above the relegation places, with the four-point deduction still applied to its total.`
        
    
    `The appeal had therefore concluded without any alteration to the Commission's original four-point sporting sanction.` — [Independent Appeal Board decision on Nottingham Forest, Premier League, 2024](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/3999776)
